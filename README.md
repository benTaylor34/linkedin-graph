Creates a html file that displats an interactive graph for linkedin connections.

# Requirements

Requires the user to submit a FOI request to linkedin to get connection data.
This can be done by doing:
* Login to your linkedin account.
* Go to Settings & Privacy: Click the "Me" icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage (your profile picture). From the dropdown, select "Settings & Privacy".
* Navigate to Data Privacy: On the left side of the settings page, click "Data privacy".
* Request Your Data: Under the section "How LinkedIn uses your data," click "Get a copy of your data".
* To download only your connections, select the "Connections" option.
* Request Archive: Click the "Request archive" button. You may be prompted to re-enter your password for security.

After completeing these steps LinkedIn will prepare your data and send a download link to your primary email address.
This process usually takes a few minutes, but it can sometimes take up to 24 hours
